FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I still need to visit an office?

For most services, no. A small number of requests need one in-person verification, and you can book that slot online.

How do I pay fees?

Pay by card, bank transfer or USSD. Every payment generates an official receipt instantly.

Is my personal data safe?

Yes. Data is stored on state-controlled infrastructure and is never shared without your consent or a legal order.
